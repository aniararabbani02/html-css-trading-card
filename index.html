Shoebill Stork

The Shoebill is known for its "machine-gun" like call, made by cluttering its bill.

  • Scientific Name: Balaeniceps rex
  • Lifespan: 35 years
  • Habitat: Swamps and East African wetlands
  • Diet: Lungfish, baby crocodiles, and frogs

Standing up to five feet tall, the Shoebill is a solitary bird that remains motionless for long periods while hunting. Its massive, shoe-shaped bill is its most striking feature, used to scoop up large prey from muddy waters.
